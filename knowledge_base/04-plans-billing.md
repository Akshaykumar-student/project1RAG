# Plans and billing

FlowDesk offers Starter, Growth, and Business plans. Starter includes up to three seats and two inboxes. Growth adds unlimited inboxes, automation rules, and standard reports. Business adds SAML SSO, audit logs, and priority support.

Subscriptions are billed monthly or annually in advance. Annual billing includes a 20% discount. Owners can change the billing cycle or download invoices from **Settings → Billing**. Taxes may be added based on the billing address.
