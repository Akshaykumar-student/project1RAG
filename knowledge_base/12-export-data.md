# Export customers and conversations

Admins and owners can export customers as CSV from **Customers → Export**. Conversation exports are available from **Reports → Exports** and include messages, timestamps, assignees, tags, and satisfaction ratings.

Exports are prepared asynchronously. A download link is emailed to the requester and expires after 24 hours. Starter plans can export the previous 90 days; Growth and Business plans can export the full available history.
