# Change plans and seat count

Only the workspace owner can change the plan or purchased seat count. Upgrades take effect immediately, and FlowDesk charges a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.

If the workspace has more active members than the new seat limit, remove members before scheduling a downgrade. Reducing purchased seats also takes effect at the next renewal; removing a teammate alone does not change the invoice.
