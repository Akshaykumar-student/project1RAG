# Invite and remove team members

Admins and owners can invite teammates from **Settings → Team → Invite member**. Enter the teammate's email address and choose the Admin, Agent, or Viewer role. Invitations expire after seven days; an admin can resend or cancel a pending invitation.

To remove a member, open their profile from the Team page and select **Remove member**. Their closed conversations remain in reports, while their open conversations become unassigned. Removing a member does not automatically reduce the purchased seat count.
