# Connect a support email inbox

Admins can connect Gmail or Microsoft 365 using OAuth from **Settings → Inboxes → Add inbox**. Other providers connect by forwarding mail to the unique FlowDesk forwarding address shown during setup. A verification message must arrive before the inbox becomes active.

FlowDesk sends replies using the connected address for OAuth inboxes. Forwarding-only inboxes send from a generated FlowDesk address unless custom SMTP is configured on the Growth or Business plan.
