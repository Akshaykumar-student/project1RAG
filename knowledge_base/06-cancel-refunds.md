# Cancel a subscription and request a refund

The workspace owner can cancel from **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period. Until then, the workspace remains fully available. After cancellation, the workspace becomes read-only for 30 days and is then permanently deleted.

Monthly payments are non-refundable. An annual subscription can receive a full refund only when requested within 14 calendar days of the initial annual purchase. Renewal payments and prorated upgrade charges are not refundable. Contact billing@flowdesk.example to request an eligible refund.
