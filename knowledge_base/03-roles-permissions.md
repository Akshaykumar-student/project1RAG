# Roles and permissions

Owners can manage billing, delete the workspace, change every setting, and transfer ownership. Admins can manage teammates, inboxes, integrations, automations, and reports, but cannot delete the workspace or transfer ownership. Agents can view and reply to conversations in assigned inboxes. Viewers have read-only access and cannot send replies.

Every workspace has exactly one owner. Ownership can be transferred only to an active Admin from **Settings → Workspace → Transfer ownership**.
