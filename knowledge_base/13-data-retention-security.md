# Data retention and security

FlowDesk encrypts data in transit using TLS and encrypts stored customer content using AES-256. Production access is restricted, logged, and reviewed. Business customers can view workspace audit logs for 365 days.

Active workspaces retain conversations until an Admin deletes them. Deleted conversations remain recoverable for 30 days and are then permanently removed. After subscription cancellation, the entire workspace is read-only for 30 days before permanent deletion.
