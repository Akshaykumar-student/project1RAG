# Reset your password

Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enter your account email. The reset link expires after 30 minutes and can be used once. If it expires, request a new link; only the newest reset email remains valid.

If no email arrives within five minutes, check spam and confirm that the address is the one used for FlowDesk. Accounts that sign in exclusively through Google or SAML do not have a FlowDesk password and must use their identity provider.
